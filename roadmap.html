The NYC Firearms Licensing Roadmap

This is the general shape of the process for an NYC handgun license, from first steps to final approval. It's a starting map, not a guarantee — your specific situation may add or skip steps, and only the NYPD can tell you exactly where your application stands.

Rifle/shotgun permits follow a related but separate NYPD process. Many of these steps still apply, but always confirm the specifics for that permit type directly with the NYPD.

1

Confirm You're Eligible

Before anything else, check the basic eligibility requirements: generally age 21+ (with a military exception), and meeting the "good moral character" standard defined in state law, without any of the specific disqualifying factors it lists.

Official source: New York Penal Law § 400.00 · Last verified: August 18, 2026

2

Choose the Right License or Permit Type

Decide which specific license or permit fits your situation — for example, a premises license (keeping a handgun at a specific address) versus a carry license, or a separate rifle/shotgun permit. The right choice affects the rest of the process, so this is worth getting right early.

Official source: NYPD License Division · Last verified: August 18, 2026

3

Gather Your Required Documents

Start collecting proof of identity, residency, and any other documentation your license type requires. Doing this early — before you're in the middle of the application — is the single biggest thing you can do to keep the process moving smoothly.

Use the document checklist →

Official source: NYPD Online Licensing Portal — instructions · Last verified: August 18, 2026

4

Prepare Your Photo

Applications require a recent photo meeting specific formatting requirements. Getting this right the first time avoids a needless delay.

Prepare your photo →

Official source: NYPD Online Licensing Portal — instructions · Last verified: August 18, 2026

5

Complete the Online Application & Pay Fees

Since 2018, the NYPD has accepted applications only through its online portal — paper applications aren't accepted. As part of this step, you'll pay the official government application fee.

See how the portal itself works →  ·  Estimate your fees →

Official source: NYPD Online Licensing Portal · Last verified: August 18, 2026

6

Fingerprinting & Background Investigation

Applicants are fingerprinted and undergo a background investigation as part of the review. For some license types, this stage can also include an in-person interview and character references.

Official source: New York Penal Law § 400.00 · Last verified: August 18, 2026

7

Wait for a Determination

By law, a licensing officer has up to six months to act on a completed application. Processing time in practice can vary — this is exactly the kind of thing worth checking directly with the NYPD rather than assuming.

Official source: New York Penal Law § 400.00 · Last verified: August 18, 2026

8

Once Approved: Ongoing Responsibilities & Renewal

Approval isn't the end of the process — you'll also take on ongoing legal responsibilities, like safe storage requirements, and your license will eventually need to be renewed through the NYPD (not the New York State recertification system, which is for permits issued outside NYC).
