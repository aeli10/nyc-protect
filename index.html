Common Questions

Is NYC Protect affiliated with the NYPD or NYC?

No. NYC Protect is an independent, educational resource. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of the NYPD, New York City, New York State, or any government agency. Official government sources always control.

Does NYC Protect give legal advice?

No. We explain what official sources say in plain English and link directly to them. For advice about your specific situation, consult a licensed attorney.

Is my information stored anywhere?

No account is required, and your checklist progress is saved only in your own browser on your own device — never on our servers.
