Photo Tool

Prepare a chest-up photo for your application. Everything happens right here in your browser — your photo is never uploaded or sent anywhere.

Choose a photo from your device, or take a new one.

This tool crops to a square, matching the general 1½ × 1½ inch chest-up format referenced in the NYPD's application instructions. Always confirm the exact current photo requirements directly with the NYPD before submitting.
