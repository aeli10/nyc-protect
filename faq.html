Frequently Asked Questions

Short, plain-English answers. For anything legal or procedural, see the linked official source.

Is NYC Protect affiliated with the NYPD, NYC, or NYS?

No. NYC Protect is an independent, educational resource. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of the NYPD, New York City, New York State, or any government agency. See our full About & Disclaimer page.

Does NYC Protect give legal advice?

No. We explain what official sources say in plain English and link directly to them. For advice about your specific situation, consult a licensed attorney.

Does NYC Protect cost anything?

No. The site is completely free, with no subscriptions, paywalls, ads, or lead-generation forms — and it never will have any of those.

Is my information stored anywhere?

No account is required. Where the site saves your progress (like the Document Checklist or the Start Here questionnaire), that information is saved only in your own browser, on your own device — never on a server, never sent to us.

What license types does NYC Protect cover?

Primarily NYC handgun licenses (premises and carry) and rifle/shotgun permits, both issued by the NYPD License Division. See Legal & Safety Concepts for how these differ.

Am I eligible for a firearms license?

Eligibility is set by New York State law — generally age 21+ (with a military exception) and meeting the "good moral character" standard, without specific disqualifying factors. We explain this on the Legal & Safety Concepts page, but only the NYPD can determine your actual eligibility.

How much does a license cost?

As of our last check, the handgun license application fee is $340, the rifle/shotgun permit fee is $140, and the fingerprint fee is $88.25 for everyone. Use our Fee Calculator for an itemized estimate, and always confirm the current amount on the official NYPD portal before paying.

How long does the process take?

By law, a licensing officer has up to six months to act on a completed application, though actual processing time can vary. This is worth confirming directly with the NYPD rather than assuming.

I live outside NYC — does this site still apply to me?

Not directly. NYC Protect is built specifically around New York City's licensing system, which is separate from the county-based system used in the rest of New York State. If you're outside NYC, your process runs through your county clerk or the New York State Police instead.

How do I renew my NYC license?

NYC licenses are renewed directly through the NYPD, not through the New York State recertification portal (that system is for permits issued outside NYC). See the NYPD Online Licensing Portal.

What if my application is denied?

NYC Protect doesn't handle appeals or denials — this is exactly the kind of situation where speaking with a licensed attorney is worthwhile. The NYPD can also explain the specific reason for a denial.

Does NYC Protect offer tactical, self-defense, or weapon-selection advice?

No, and it never will. This site covers licensing, legal compliance, safety concepts, and paperwork only.
