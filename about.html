What NYC Protect Is

NYC Protect is a free, independent website built to make the New York City firearms licensing process easier to understand. The process is notoriously confusing — spread across multiple agencies, forms, and fee schedules — and this site exists to translate that into plain English, without charging for it or trying to sell you anything.

Specifically, this site helps you:

  • Understand the official requirements for various NYC firearms licenses
  • Organize the paperwork and documentation the process requires
  • Understand official government fees before you owe them
  • Understand the general shape and order of the application process
  • Find the correct official NYPD and New York State resources for your situation
  • Prepare administrative materials, like a properly formatted photo

What NYC Protect Is Not

To keep this a safe, clearly educational and administrative resource, there are firm limits on what you'll find here:

  • Not legal advice. We explain what official sources say and link to them; we never tell you whether you personally are eligible or will be approved. For advice about your specific situation, consult a licensed attorney.
  • Not tactical, self-defense, weapon-selection, or ammunition guidance. This site covers licensing, legal compliance, safety, and paperwork only — never shooting technique, defensive tactics, or product recommendations.
  • Not a substitute for official sources. Every legal or procedural claim on this site links to the government page or document it came from.
  • Not a place that sells anything. No subscriptions, no paywalls, no advertising, and no forms that hand your information to a dealer or training company.

Your Privacy

No account is required to use NYC Protect. Where the site lets you check off documents or track progress, that information is saved only in your own browser, on your own device — it is never sent to us or stored on a server. We do not collect sensitive personal information, and we don't need to, since we're not the ones deciding your application.

Questions About This Site

NYC Protect is an independent project, not a government help desk — for questions about your specific application, always contact the NYPD License Division or New York State directly through the links on our Official Resources page.
